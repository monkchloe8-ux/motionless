What this cannot tell you

These are the settings people left behind, nothing more. The site does not know whether someone found a movement uncomfortable, only where they stopped the slider. It also cannot tell the difference between a considered choice and a slider nobody touched, except that untouched sliders are recorded separately.

One of the four movements, the flow, answers your cursor. A phone has no cursor, so on a touch screen it is a milder thing than on a laptop. Which kind of device each visitor used is recorded alongside their answer, so the two can be told apart.
