Before you start

There are four sliders. Where you leave them is your answer.

  1. Each section below shows the same short paragraph, with a different kind of movement around it. Read it. At the end you will be asked one question about what it said.
  2. Everything starts still. Next to each one is a slider that adds motion. Turn it up until the movement is as much as you would actually want on a website.
  3. Leave the slider there. That position is what gets recorded and published. There is no right answer.

Some sections move a great deal and may feel uncomfortable. You can stop at any time.

Read this while it moves.

A screen can trigger the same response as real movement. Your eyes report motion your body cannot feel, and that mismatch is enough to produce nausea, headache and a loss of focus. The same words are set in all four of these. What changes is what the motion is doing to them, and how much of this you can still take in.
